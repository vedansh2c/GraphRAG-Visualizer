Sherlock Holmes and John Watson worked from Baker Street to investigate a tunnel beneath London Bank. London Bank asked Inspector Lestrade to protect its gold reserves. Inspector Lestrade met Sherlock Holmes and John Watson at Baker Street to review the evidence.
Jabez Wilson owned a small shop near London Bank. Vincent Spaulding worked for Jabez Wilson and secretly surveyed London Bank. Sherlock Holmes observed clay on Vincent Spaulding's trousers and connected the shop to the tunnel. John Watson recorded that Jabez Wilson was lured away from his shop by a false employment offer.
Inspector Lestrade arrested Vincent Spaulding inside London Bank after Sherlock Holmes predicted the timing of the robbery. John Watson described the bank case as an example of using a small physical clue to expose an organized plan. London Bank recovered its gold reserves.
