Henry Baskerville arrived at Baskerville Hall with Doctor Mortimer. Doctor Mortimer warned Henry Baskerville about a dangerous hound on Dartmoor. Henry Baskerville asked Barrymore to explain the lights seen near Baskerville Hall.
Beryl Stapleton warned Henry Baskerville to leave Dartmoor. Jack Stapleton kept a large hound hidden near Dartmoor and planned to frighten Henry Baskerville. Doctor Mortimer studied the footprints outside Baskerville Hall and found physical evidence of the hound.
Barrymore helped Henry Baskerville understand the paths across Dartmoor. Beryl Stapleton exposed Jack Stapleton's scheme and rescued Henry Baskerville from danger. Doctor Mortimer explained that the feared supernatural story concealed a deliberate crime motivated by inheritance.
